## Information handled by the app

Text Quran does not require an account and has no advertising or analytics SDKs. Reader preferences and bookmarks are stored in the app's private local storage. Downloaded Quran text is cached there for offline reading. The app does not send those preferences, bookmarks, or cached files to its developer.

When the user downloads or refreshes an edition, the app makes an HTTPS request to AlQuran Cloud for the selected Quran text. The request identifies the requested public edition; it does not include the user's name, account, bookmarks, or reader settings. The service operator may receive the device's IP address and ordinary request/network metadata. AlQuran Cloud's published [terms](https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions) describe per-IP rate limiting and the origins/attribution of Quran text, but do not set out a detailed privacy policy. Review the service's current terms before use or publication.

## Backups and retention

Local preferences, bookmarks, and cached text remain on the device until removed by the user or the app is uninstalled. Android system/device backup may include app data according to the user's device and backup settings; Text Quran does not operate that backup service.

## Sharing, security, and children's privacy

The app has no developer-operated account system and does not sell user information. Quran text is requested from AlQuran Cloud over HTTPS. No data transmission over the public internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Choices and contact

Users can change or reset reader preferences in the app. Resetting preferences does not remove cached Quran text or bookmarks; those can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. For privacy questions, contact: [Add the developer's monitored contact email before publication].
