# Google Play listing draft

## App name

Text Quran

## Short description

Read Quran text on your Wear OS watch, with Arabic and English editions.

## Full description

Read the Quran directly on your Wear OS watch. Text Quran provides the Arabic Uthmani edition and the Saheeh International English translation.

Features:

- Read downloaded text offline after the first successful download.
- Choose Arabic or English, dark or light theme, text color, font family, font size, and bold text.
- Scroll manually or use adjustable-speed auto-scroll.
- Turn pages with a swipe or a wrist gesture.
- Save and revisit bookmarked pages.
- Choose a symbol to display with each ayah's number.

Quran text editions are provided through AlQuran Cloud. An internet connection is needed to download or refresh an edition.
