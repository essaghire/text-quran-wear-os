Text Quran

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This policy describes the Text Quran app for Wear OS.

Information handled by the app

Text Quran does not require an account and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs. Reader preferences and bookmarks are stored in the app's private local storage. Downloaded Quran text is cached there for offline reading. The app does not send those preferences, bookmarks, or cached files to its developer.

When you download or refresh an edition, the app makes an HTTPS request to AlQuran Cloud for the selected Quran text. The request identifies the requested public edition; it does not include your name, account, bookmarks, or reader settings. The service operator may receive your device's IP address and ordinary request/network metadata. AlQuran Cloud's published terms describe per-IP rate limiting and the origins/attribution of Quran text. The service is operated independently; review its current terms for its handling of request data.

Backups and retention

Local preferences, bookmarks, and cached text remain on your device until removed by you or the app is uninstalled. Android system/device backup may include app data according to your device and backup settings; Text Quran does not operate that backup service.

Sharing and security

The app has no developer-operated account system and does not sell user information. The selected Quran text is requested from AlQuran Cloud over HTTPS. No data transmission over the public internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and contact

You can change or reset reader preferences in the app. Resetting preferences does not remove cached Quran text or bookmarks; those can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

For privacy questions, contact essaghir.e@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices or included services change.
