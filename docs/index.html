Text Quran

Quran reading for Wear OS

Read on your watch

Text Quran is a standalone Wear OS reader with Arabic Uthmani text and the Saheeh International English translation. Choose your text appearance, scroll at your own pace, and save bookmarks for later.

Text editions are requested from AlQuran Cloud. Arabic source attribution: Tanzil.net and Quran Academy. English translation: Saheeh International.

Privacy

Reader settings, bookmarks, and downloaded text are stored locally on your watch. When downloading text, the request goes to AlQuran Cloud, which may receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata.
